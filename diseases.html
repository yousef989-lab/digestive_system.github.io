Disease Info On Disease
Gastroesophageal Reflux Disease (GERD) A chronic condition where the stomach acid frequently flows back into the esophagus, causing constant heartburn, along with the potential damage to the esophageal lining.
Irritable Bowel Syndrome (IBS) A common disorder of the large intestine. Symptoms include cramping, abdominal pain, bloating, gas, and changes in bowel habits (frequent diarrhea or constipation).
